VIDEO RECORDING GUIDE — What to show for each audio clip
==========================================================

STEP 1: Record all voiceover clips first (audio only)
STEP 2: Record screen video silently (following actions below)
STEP 3: In editor, place audio clips on timeline matching the actions


SETUP BEFORE RECORDING:
- Start Ollama: OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0 ollama serve
- Connect phone: adb reverse tcp:11434 tcp:11434
- Open scrcpy: scrcpy --record firstvoice-demo.mp4
- Pre-warm model: open app, do one quick test, then clear data
- App should be fresh (no old encounters)


VIDEO ACTIONS (record silently, match to audio clips later):
=============================================================

CLIP 1+2 (0:00-0:24) — Opening
ACTION: Show phone home screen with FirstVoice app open.
        Show "Ollama Connected" status on home screen.
        Maybe briefly show the feature icons on home screen.


CLIP 3 (0:24-0:29) — Start encounter
ACTION: Tap "New Encounter" button.
        Encounter screen opens with empty chat area.


CLIP 4+5 (0:29-0:56) — Audio encoder
ACTION: Tap SURVIVOR mic button (turns red, shows "Recording...")
        Speak Hindi into the phone: "मुझे पानी चाहिए, मेरा बच्चा बीमार है"
        Tap button again to stop (turns back to normal)
        Show loading: "Transcribing with Gemma 4..."
        Wait for result to appear (SPEED THIS UP IN EDIT to 3-4s)
        Hindi text appears in survivor bubble
        English translation appears below it


CLIP 6 (0:56-1:01) — Result
ACTION: Camera stays on the chat bubble showing:
        - Hindi original text
        - "Language: Hindi" 
        - English translation below


CLIP 7 (1:01-1:08) — TTS
ACTION: Tap the 🔊 speaker icon on the translated text
        Phone speaks aloud (you'll hear it in screen recording)
        Then tap the 👂 ear icon to show that works too


CLIP 8 (1:08-1:18) — Responder replies
ACTION: Tap RESPONDER mic button (turns red)
        Speak English: "How many people are with you? Is anyone injured?"
        Tap to stop
        Show loading "Transcribing with Gemma 4..."
        (SPEED UP IN EDIT)
        English text appears in responder bubble
        Hindi translation appears
        Tap 🔊 speaker icon — phone speaks Hindi


CLIP 9 (1:18-1:28) — Photo
ACTION: Tap Camera button
        Take a photo (of a disaster image on another screen, or printed photo)
        Show loading "Analyzing photo with AI..."
        (SPEED UP IN EDIT)
        Assessment appears in chat: severity, hazards, summary


CLIP 10 (1:28-1:40) — Triage card
ACTION: Tap "End Encounter" (checkmark button)
        Confirm dialog appears → tap "End & Generate Triage Card"
        Show loading "Generating triage card..."
        (SPEED UP IN EDIT)
        Toast shows urgency level
        Navigate to Triage Cards screen
        Show the generated card — PAUSE 3 SECONDS on it
        (Let judges read: urgency, people count, needs, summary)


CLIP 11+12 (1:40-1:58) — Mesh sync
ACTION: [SWITCH TO SPLIT SCREEN — two phones]
        Phone 1: Shows triage cards list with the card we just made
        Phone 2: Open FirstVoice, go to Mesh Sync screen
        Show: peer discovered, sync in progress
        Phone 2: Navigate to Triage Cards — same card appears!
        (If you can't do split screen live, record Phone 2 separately
         and edit as picture-in-picture)


CLIP 13 (1:58-2:13) — Dashboard + features
ACTION: Open Dashboard screen — show map with pins
        Tap a pin to show card details
        Switch to timeline view
        Go back to Triage Cards screen
        Show export button (share sheet with JSON/CSV)
        Show recycle bin icon + badge
        Quick flash of Field Radio / Walkie-Talkie screen


CLIP 14 (2:13-2:23) — Future
ACTION: Show Settings screen briefly (Ollama URL configurable)
        Or just stay on home screen while voiceover plays


CLIP 15 (2:23-2:38) — Closing
ACTION: Show home screen with all features visible
        Or show a final title card:
        "FirstVoice | Gemma 4 E4B | Apache 2.0"
        "github.com/[YOUR_REPO]"
